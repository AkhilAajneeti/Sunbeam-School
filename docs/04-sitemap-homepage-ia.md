# F. Proposed Sitemap

## F1. Navigation architecture

**Main navigation — 8 items.** The audit forces two of them (Career §6.1, Alumni §12.1, adjacent). The rest follow the parent's decision sequence.

```
About   Academics   Admissions   Campus   Beyond Academics   News & Events   Alumni   Career
```

**Utility bar — above main nav, for existing parents and students:**

```
Notices · Academic Calendar · Results · Download TC · Parent Login · Mandatory Public Disclosure · Contact      [ Apply Online ▾ ]
```

### Decisions and their reasons

| Decision | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Career promoted to top level** | Audit 6.1, verbatim |
| **Alumni promoted to top level, beside Career** | Audit 12.1, verbatim — the two sit adjacent as specified |
| **"Essential Information" abolished** | A junk drawer of six unrelated items. Career → top level; Calendar → Academics (audit 8.1); Bus Routes → Campus; Uniform → Admissions; Golden Rules → About; Press Release → News & Events |
| **"School Activities" folded into Beyond Academics** | Two overlapping top-level items today. Audit §5 asks for better presentation, not separate navigation |
| **Chairman's / Secretary's messages removed from nav** | Audit 1.11, verbatim — absorbed into History & Legacy |
| **Director's and Principal's messages kept as pages** | Audit 1.9 and 1.10, verbatim |
| **"Beyond Academics" retained as the label** | The school's own language and the audit's. Not renamed to "Student Life" |
| **Facilities & Infrastructure merged into Campus** | Audit 3.9, verbatim |
| **Mandatory Public Disclosure in utility bar *and* footer** | Regulatory findability |
| **8 items, not 9+** | Mega-menus carry depth; the top bar carries clarity |

### Mega-menu behaviour

**About, Academics, Admissions, Campus, Beyond Academics** open a full-width mega-menu (2–4 columns, one featured image, one contextual CTA). **News & Events, Alumni, Career** are direct links with a small dropdown or none — they are shallow by design.

Every mega-menu column header is itself a **link to a real landing page.** No orphan headings, no dead parents — the primary failure of the current menu.

# G. Homepage Information Architecture

**Principle:** the homepage is an *answer sequence*, not a content dump. Every section closes one parent question and opens the next (journey E1). A section earns its place only if it answers a question the previous section created.

## G1. Section order

| # | Section | Answers | Why it exists |
|---|---|---|---|
| 01 | **Utility Bar** | "I'm an existing parent — where's the notice?" | Serves D2/D3 *before* the marketing begins. Keeps returning users out of the sales funnel entirely |
| 02 | **Main Navigation** | "What does this school contain?" | Institutional structure at a glance + a permanently visible Admissions CTA |
| 03 | **Hero** | "What is Sunbeam Ballia?" | Audit 1.1–1.3. One cinematic photograph of the real campus, one confident line, two CTAs. **Stationary slider — images cross-fade, the frame and text never move (1.3)** |
| 03b | **Recognition proof strip** | "Is it actually good?" | Thin band under the hero: Education World **#1 Co-Ed Day School of Ballia, six consecutive years** · CBSE 2131962 · Microsoft Showcase School · Est. 2013. **Not a section** — a proof bar. Answers the trust question in the first viewport, while the audit's full Achievements section stays at §14 as instructed |
| 04 | **Parent Quick Access** | "Where's the thing I came for?" | Admissions · Transport · Calendar · Notices · Results · Contact. Six links, restrained, no icons-for-decoration. Serves D2 in one tap |
| 05 | **The Sunbeam Story** | "Who are they? Can I trust them?" | **Audit 1.6, 1.7, 1.8 — history, vision, mission, legacy.** Editorial storytelling: 1972 Varanasi → 2013 Ballia → 456 to 2,200+. Verified statistics only |
| 06 | **Academic Journey** | "What will my child study?" | Audit 2.B — five stages, Pre-Primary to Senior Secondary, plus streams. The first *substantive* answer for a prospective parent |
| 07 | **Learning at Sunbeam** | "How do they teach? Is it modern?" | Audit 2.A + 2.C — experiential and inquiry-based learning, STEM, robotics, AI & digital literacy, labs. Evidenced by Microsoft Showcase status, not asserted |
| 08 | **Campus Experience** | "What is the campus like?" | **Audit 3.2–3.7** — labelled: Shooting Range, Auditorium, Conference Room, Science Laboratories, Library, Sports. Photography-led at full scale, not thumbnails |
| 09 | **Beyond Academics** | "Is there more than exams?" | **Audit 4.3–4.7** — sports *participation* first, then arts, clubs, leadership, excursions. Deliberately inverts the current achievements-first framing |
| 10 | **Life at Sunbeam** | "What is it actually like to be a child here?" | Audit 1.4 — immersive full-bleed photography, entry to **dedicated event galleries**. The emotional centre; the only section whose job is feeling, not information |
| 11 | **News, Events & Notices** | "Is this school alive right now?" | Audit §10 + §11 — three visually distinct streams. Recency is the message; a stale block here does active harm |
| 12 | **Alumni — From Sunbeam to the World** | "What happens to students afterwards?" | **Audit §13** — dedicated homepage testimonial section, video-led. Doubles as the strongest outcome proof available to a prospective parent |
| 13 | **Affiliations & Partners** | "Who vouches for them?" | **Audit 1.13** — logos, cleanly displayed, immediately before the tail |
| 14 | **Achievements & Recognition** | "Show me the evidence." | **Audit 1.14** — follows §13 exactly as instructed. Replaces the Google Reviews block (audit 1.5) |
| 15 | **Admissions CTA** | "How do I start?" | Emotional close, three commitment levels: enquire · visit · apply |
| 16 | **Footer** | "Where's everything else?" | **Audit 14.1** — structured, compact, small monochrome social icons |

## G4. Rhythm

Section width and treatment must vary or the page reads as a template. Sequence:

```
§03 FULL BLEED cinematic  →  §03b thin contained strip  →  §04 contained utility band
§05 asymmetric editorial (text left / image right, offset)
§06 contained 5-column horizontal scale
§07 FULL BLEED split — image left, dark panel right
§08 FULL BLEED asymmetric photo mosaic, labelled
§09 contained, uneven 2:1 editorial grid
§10 FULL BLEED immersive band  →  breathing whitespace
§11 contained 3-stream layout
§12 FULL BLEED dark, video-led
§13 contained quiet logo row
§14 contained, large numerals + photography
§15 FULL BLEED image with dark scrim
§16 dark footer
```

Five full-bleed moments, seven contained. **No two adjacent sections share a background treatment or a column count.** This is what prevents the "AI-generated website" look far more reliably than any styling rule.
